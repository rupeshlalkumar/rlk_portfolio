# ENTREPRENEUR PORTFOLIO WEBSITE

## Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

### Version 1.0 to Version 5.0

**Website:** Rupesh Lal Kumar
**Positioning:** Entrepreneur | Business Builder
**Tagline:** Think. Build. Sell. Grow.

---

# 1. PURPOSE OF THIS SOP

This SOP defines the complete development and growth system for the **Rupesh Lal Kumar Entrepreneur Portfolio Website**, from the initial personal portfolio to a mature entrepreneurial digital ecosystem.

The website will progressively evolve through five major versions:

**V1 → Foundation**
**V2 → Dynamic Platform**
**V3 → Content & Growth Platform**
**V4 → Business Ecosystem**
**V5 → Entrepreneurial Digital Hub**

The objective is to avoid building everything at once. Each version should solve a specific business and technical requirement before the next version begins.

---
