# 1. V1 Final Goal

The purpose of V1 is simple:

> **Create a professional, complete, responsive personal website that clearly introduces Rupesh Lal Kumar as an Entrepreneur | Business Builder and presents his businesses, projects, journey, content direction, and contact pathway.**

V1 should answer five questions immediately:

```text
1. Who is Rupesh Lal Kumar?
              ↓
2. What does he do?
              ↓
3. What businesses does he operate?
              ↓
4. What has he built?
              ↓
5. How can someone connect with him?
```

V1 is **not** an admin platform.

V1 is **not** a CMS.

V1 is **not** a database-driven system.

V1 is the professional public foundation.
